Lexivo is a free and open-source (FOSS) cross-platform word puzzle game built with Rust and egui/eframe. It challenges players to solve linguistic puzzles by transforming words through single-letter changes and anagrams.

Key Features:
- Cross-platform Word Puzzles: Play seamlessly across Android, Web (PWA) and Desktop.
- Engaging Gameplay: Transform words via single-letter changes and anagrams.
- Daily Challenges: Compete in daily puzzle sets with stable global seeds.
- Quick Play: Endless word fun with randomised puzzles.
- Difficulty Levels: Choose between Easy, Medium and Hard pools.
- Privacy-first: All data is stored locally on your device with no tracking or ads.
- Lightweight & Reactive: A fast interface powered by the egui toolkit.

Lexivo is built with a commitment to FOSS values, providing a clean, ad-free experience for word game enthusiasts.
